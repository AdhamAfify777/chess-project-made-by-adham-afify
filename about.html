My Chess Journey

I started playing chess for the challenge and enjoyment of the game. Over time, chess became a passion and taught me the value of patience, discipline, calculation, and creative thinking.

As I continued improving, I wanted to share what I learned with other players. That is the idea behind MovexaChess Academy: to explain chess concepts clearly and help players work towards their goals.

I believe improvement starts with understanding, not just memorizing moves. Every game is an opportunity to learn, recognize mistakes, and make better decisions.
